# Cover letter, round 3

Dear Editor,

We submit the third revised version of manuscript aa52082-24, "Characterizing NGC 6383: A study of fundamental properties, pre-main sequence stars, mass segregation, and age using Gaia DR3 and 2MASS."

We thank the referee for recommending publication, and we have adopted both remaining suggestions; the point-by-point response is enclosed. Sect. 6.1 now states the R_V = 3.1 and the extinction law adopted in the isochrone fit, and gives the corresponding E(B-V) for comparison with the literature values of Table A.1, with the in-cluster support for a normal extinction law found by van den Ancker et al. (2000); the footnote of Table 1 and the caption of Fig. B.4 refer to it. Sect. 6.2 now describes where the Sagitta age scale comes from: its labels are empirical, and tracing them to the models underneath shows a literature-anchored compilation rather than a single theoretical PMS grid, whose offsets against MIST, PARSEC, Baraffe et al. (2015), and spotted models have been measured by Kounkel et al. (2023).

Tracing that provenance, we found and corrected four points, all disclosed in the response letter: NGC 6383 is itself in the Sagitta training set, labeled 2 Myr from the literature; the 0.2 dex offset between the median Sagitta age and our isochrone age is comparable to the 0.14 dex offset between the Sagitta and MIST scales, so Sects. 3.1 and 6.1 no longer treat the Sagitta age as independent evidence of an older age; a sentence attributed to Kounkel et al. (2023) described a comparison broader than the one that paper contains and has been restated with its actual scope; and a sentence asserting that no effect in the literature pushes the age younger has been restricted to the effects we identified. We also corrected a count in the footnote of Table 1 (four remaining rows, not three) and the stellar population and age to which a result of Feiden (2016) applies. Finally, checking the reasoning behind the age interval, we found that the manuscript never stated where its lower bar of -0.20 dex came from; Sect. 6.1 now states that it is a judgment and why the PMS locus cannot set it, and gives the measured fraction of PMS candidates enclosed by the isochrones it previously described as bracketing the locus; the corresponding 1.6-6.3 Myr range is described in the same terms in Table 1, the abstract, and the conclusions. No tabulated value or conclusion changes. Six references were added.

As requested, we checked the figure and table captions against the A&A author guide and language guide, and revised them where they departed from it. Table titles are now short, with the additional description moved to table notes (Tables 1, 2, A.1, D.1, and D.2); the units in Table A.1 are given in parentheses below the column headings; interpretive sentences already stated in the main text were removed from the captions of Figs. 5, 7, 10, B.3, B.4, and D.1, and descriptive padding from Fig. C.4; the captions of Figs. 8 and 9 now open with a descriptive phrase, and panel descriptions in Figs. 9 and C.1 no longer open with an article; the column headings of Table A.1 are in sentence case with E(B-V) set as a symbol; the panel labels of Fig. B.3 follow those of the other figures; word symbols in the caption of Fig. B.4 are set in roman; emphasis by italics was removed from the footnote of Table 1; and "i.e." is followed by a comma in the caption of Fig. C.5. The captions of Figs. 8, 9, and D.1 now describe their line styles and symbols. Each appendix figure and table now appears within its own appendix, which adds three pages. We also regenerated nine figures from their scripts, with unchanged data, to correct the text inside them: the key of Fig. 6 says "Ensemble draws" rather than "Posterior draws", matching the caption and Sect. 3.1; the axis labels of Fig. B.4 use the symbols of its caption; the key of Fig. 2 marks the adopted distance as the mode, and that of Fig. 3 no longer uses C, the concentration parameter of Table 1, for the proper-motion center; the keys and axis labels of Figs. 9, B.1, C.3, C.4, and C.5 are in sentence case (with "Optimal" replaced by "Adopted" in Fig. B.1 and "Center" by "Cluster center" in Fig. C.3, the terms of their captions), and the mass ranges of Figs. 9 and C.5 carry their unit.

The author list is unchanged from the previous version: no coauthors have been added or removed, and the order is unchanged (L.M. Pulgar-Escobar, N.A. Henriquez-Salgado, R.E. Mennickent, P. Cerulo).

Before resubmitting we ran the checks listed in your letter. The spelling is consistently US throughout, including the new text. The one abbreviation introduced in the new text, MWSC, is expanded at its first occurrence. The six new references follow the A&A style, like the rest of the list.

The upload contains the clean revised source, the marked-change PDF (latexdiff against the previously submitted revision, so only the changes of this round are highlighted), and the point-by-point response. The CDS data archive is unchanged from the previous revision; we enclose it again for completeness.

Sincerely,

L.M. Pulgar-Escobar, on behalf of the authors
