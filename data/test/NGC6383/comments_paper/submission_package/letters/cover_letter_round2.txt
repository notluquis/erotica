# Cover letter, round 2

Dear Editor,

We submit the second revised version of manuscript aa52082-24, "Characterizing NGC 6383: A study of fundamental properties, pre-main sequence stars, mass segregation, and age using Gaia DR3 and 2MASS."

We thank the referee for the second report. We agree with their central criticism, that the previous organization made the paper hard to read, and have reorganized the manuscript accordingly. The paper is now structured thematically (Data; Analysis framework and cluster membership; Astrometric parameters; Cluster structure; Age, extinction, and stellar content; Luminosity, mass, and dynamical state; Comparison with previous studies), so that each result immediately follows the description of the method that produces it, every figure and table sits next to the text that discusses it, and a new overview paragraph states the purpose of each tool and the logic connecting the analysis steps. All seventeen specific points are addressed in the enclosed point-by-point response, which opens with a mapping between the old and new section numbers.

The main scientific change follows the referee's suggestion on the extraction radius: the King outer radius is now measured on the widest (70 arcmin) extraction, the only one whose radial profile reaches a fitted background annulus beyond the cluster, giving R_t = 54 (+7/-11) arcmin; the core radius and all membership-based quantities remain tied to the 40 arcmin reference catalog, because the behavior of the wider runs is consistent with additional field contamination. The dependence of all quoted R_t intervals on the physically motivated prior bound is now stated explicitly, together with a relaxed-bound sensitivity refit.

While re-verifying every numerical claim for this revision we also found and corrected, on our own initiative, three errors inherited from the originally submitted version, all disclosed in the response letter and visible in the marked-up PDF: the half-mass relaxation-time coefficient (0.17 -> 0.138, the standard form of Spitzer 1987; t_rh = 24.7 +/- 7.6 Myr), the Galactic enclosed mass used for the Hill radius (now from the McMillan 2017 potential; R_Hill = 33.6 +/- 2.2 arcmin), and the distance-modulus uncertainty (a duplicated extinction uncertainty; now +/-0.09 mag). None of these changes affects any conclusion; none weakens the dynamical-youth argument. Re-checking Kalari (2019) against its body text rather than its abstract, while addressing the referee's point on the provenance of the literature ages, produced four further corrections to our own text, also disclosed in the response letter: his age is a mean and not a median, his mass range is 0.3-0.9 rather than 0.3-1.0 Msun, his quoted uncertainty is the error on the mean rather than the dispersion, and our comparison had omitted that he adopts a distance 0.41 mag larger in distance modulus than ours, which biases his ages young relative to ours. Our earlier attribution of the difference to the choice of evolutionary code alone was too strong and has been withdrawn.

One change is worth flagging because the referee's report refers to the software by its former name. The pipeline called COSMIC in the previous version is now called EROTICA (Estimation, Recovery & Optimization, together with Inference, for Cluster Analysis). It was renamed to avoid a collision with the unrelated cosmic-popsynth binary-population code, which shares the import name. This is a rename only: the code, the version used and the results are the same. The revised Sect. 3.1 states the former name and the reason at first mention, and the software is now cited by its archived Zenodo DOI (10.5281/zenodo.21769959) rather than by a repository link, with the repository at github.com/notluquis/erotica.

The author list is unchanged from the previous version: no coauthors have been added or removed, and the order is unchanged (L.M. Pulgar-Escobar, N.A. Henriquez-Salgado, R.E. Mennickent, P. Cerulo).

Before resubmitting we ran the three checks listed in your letter. The spelling is consistently US throughout (the single British form, "catalogue", occurs only inside the CDS-mandated VizieR acknowledgement). Every abbreviation is now expanded at its first occurrence in the main text; this revision fixes seven that were previously defined late or not at all (PMS, YSO, CMD, KS, CTTS, MIST and HDBSCAN), and one used only once (the initial mass function) is now spelled out with the abbreviation dropped. The references follow the A&A style; the three arXiv entries are works that have no journal version.

The upload contains the clean revised source, the marked-change PDF (latexdiff against the previously submitted revision, so only round-2 changes are highlighted; added text in blue, removed text in red at a smaller size, as its first page states), and the point-by-point response. The CDS data file is byte-identical to the previous submission, but we also upload a replacement dataset archive: validating the ReadMe against its own data file turned up two format defects, now corrected (the null-value marker was missing on the three 2MASS magnitude columns, each of which carries 88 null entries, and one column label was misaligned), and the archive previously uploaded additionally nested its contents in a subfolder and carried two files CDS does not require. No tabulated value changed.

Sincerely,

L.M. Pulgar-Escobar, on behalf of the authors
